Defense committee, confirmed by Shaked 2026-08-20: **Rivi (Riva) Tauman**, **Michal Kahn**, and
**Yuval Nir** (supervisor). All three are sleep researchers, which is why the deck's background now starts
at spindle subtypes and topography instead of explaining N2 (see [[project_defense_deck]]).

- **Rivi Tauman** — Sieratzki-Sagol Institute for Sleep Medicine, TASMC; clinical sleep medicine and PSG,
  mostly OSA and sleep-disordered breathing (much of it paediatric). She is **a co-author on Sharon et al.
  2025** and led its sleep-breathing assessment, and she guided the PSG monitoring for this thesis. Expect:
  the AHI ≤ 15 inclusion (mild OSA is in the sample and is not covariate-adjusted, and AHI is missing for
  some recordings), scoring quality, respiratory arousals as a confound for a measure built on
  micro-arousal dynamics, and **why slow-wave synchrony tracked impairment in her own paper while the ISFS
  did not**.
- **Michal Kahn** — School of Psychological Sciences, TAU (PhD under Avi Sadeh), Kahn Sleep Lab;
  developmental and clinical sleep, multi-method by design (actigraphy, auto-videosomnography, PSG),
  behavioural intervention trials. Expect measurement questions: one night per participant with no
  test-retest estimate, what a null means at these sample sizes, the developmental angle (the reference
  framework is Dimitriades' developmental study), links to anything behavioural, and aMCI-group
  heterogeneity.
- **Yuval Nir** — supervisor; mechanism and the LC-NE account.

Full profiles, the priority answers and the standard question set live in **`thesis/defense/qa_prep.md`**.
The single highest-priority item there is [[reference_champetier_isfs_aging]].
